# Solar Arcade priorities

What matters now, in order. The persona panel and the feedback merit check judge proposals against this list.

1. **Delight in under ten seconds on a phone.** Someone opening the page should see something playful about the
   planets right away and want to tap it.
2. **True scale, made tangible.** Sizes, distances and times are real (from `data/planets.json`) and shown so they
   land: comparisons, "how many Earths", things you can feel.
3. **Small games that teach.** Quick guessing and comparing games with instant feedback beat long text.
4. **Accessible and fast.** Readable contrast and text size, labels for every control, works without animation,
   no external assets.

Not now: accounts, saving progress, sound, new data sources, moons and dwarf planets, desktop-only layouts.
